Oltrarno

The Hidden Artisans of Santo Spirito

Real makers. Authentic crafts.

Cross the Arno into the Oltrarno, where Florence still works with its hands. In the lanes around Piazza Santo Spirito, workshop doors stand open and you can smell the leather and hear the tap of tools. We step inside to meet the local artisans who still make perfume, handbags, shoes and fashion the slow way, the way Florentines have for centuries. Find a treasure you love, and buy it from the person who made it.

2 to 4 hours · Private or small groups · In English

Across the city

Florence Wine Window Tour

Small windows. Big stories.

Join me as we explore some of Florence’s renowned wine windows, or buchette del vino. These tiny centuries-old openings were built into the walls of Florentine palazzi, allowing noble families to sell wine from their vineyards directly to people on the street. Today, a handful have come back to life, offering a charming glimpse into Florence’s past and, of course, a glass or two of wine along the way.

2 hours · Private or small groups · In English

Hands on

Workshops

Made in Florence. Treasured at home.

Roll up your sleeves and learn from Florence’s makers. Blend your own fragrance, shape clay on the wheel, craft a piece of jewellery, swirl colour into marbled paper, stitch a leather wallet or try your hand at silk weaving. In the kitchen, roll fresh pasta by hand and layer a classic tiramisù, with a glass of wine to enjoy while you cook. You leave with more than a souvenir: something you made yourself, and a story to go with it.

Around Florence

Experiences

Florence, your way.

Whether it’s gliding by e-bike through the hills of Toscana, riding horseback past cypress and vine, or following a truffle dog through the autumn woods, I’ll guide you to the experience of a lifetime. Slip away for a day to soak in thermal springs and mud baths, wander the painted villages of the Cinque Terre, browse Florence’s markets with me for flowers, produce and local treasures, sip your way through family wineries and linger over long lunches in country trattorie. Back in the city, we taste authentic Florentine cuisine the way locals do, from schiacciata eaten at a street window to slow dinners in a neighbourhood osteria. These are the moments you carry home, and remember for the rest of your life.
